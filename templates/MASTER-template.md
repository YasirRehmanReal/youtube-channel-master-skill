## 4. Production system (standing standard)

- Script sizing: words = target minutes × measured narration pace (wpm).
  Current measured pace: <wpm> (re-measure after every finished video).
- Narration: voice <name>, speed <x>, chunks ≤2,000 words, every chunk
  Whisper-verified (coverage ≥0.88, largest missing run ≤40 words). Stems
  backed up to <backup location>.
- Visuals: ≥10–12 real clips + scene-matched images; free stock first
  (Pexels/Pixabay/Wikimedia/LOC, CC0/CC-BY/PD only); AI stills fallback only.
  Places/objects/documents — follow your niche's imagery rules.
- Reference-clip islands: narrator silent, clip full volume, source-labelled,
  kept short (reused-content rule).
- Pacing: ~3s cuts (~18 cuts/min), no still >5s, dry hard cuts, no whoosh,
  one low boom per chapter boundary, one red flash at the biggest pivot.
- Music: real track only (NCS / YouTube Audio Library / CC-BY), ~12 dB under
  narration, silent in islands, never noise beds.
- Subtitles: karaoke from real word timings, large/high-contrast; SRT uploaded.
- Render: 4K (3840×2160), −16 LUFS.
- QA: full-frame sweep of the whole timeline before handover — mandatory.
- Handover: MP4 + thumbnail + SRT + upload pack, backed up.

## 5. Quality bar (standing rules)

- Hooks validated with hookscore before recording.
- First 2 minutes excellent. Subtitles better than auto-caption style.
- Disclaimer + sources + music credits in every description.
- Videos must feel real, not AI — keep the feedback log, fix causes.
- Verify every script against previous scripts before recording.
