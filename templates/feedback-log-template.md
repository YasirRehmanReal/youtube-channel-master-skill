# Viewer Feedback Log

Running log of viewer feedback about how the videos FEEL — especially anything
about AI-ness, fakeness, or quality. Standing rule: videos must FEEL REAL,
not AI. Log every signal, note the cause, fix it in the next build so the
mistake doesn't repeat.

Handling rule: bait comments (short dismissive one-liners) are NOT replied to
(see yt-comment skill) — but they ARE logged here as a signal.

## What "feel real" means in production

- Real footage/photos first (Pexels/Pixabay/Wikimedia/LOC/archives); AI stills
  only as fallback, never anything that looks generated (wrong hands, plastic
  skin, impossible details).
- Real, source-labelled reference clips in every video.
- Narration sounds human: natural pacing, no robotic flatness, no
  mispronounced names.
- Varied visuals: no long stretches of the same still, no repeated shots.
- No blank/broken frames, no frozen zooms (full-frame QA on every build).
